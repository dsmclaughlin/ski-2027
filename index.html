On paper La Plagne is £240 more per person. In practice it's about the same, or even cheaper. Les Deux Alpes only includes breakfast, so a week of dinners out at ~£40 a head puts it at ~£1,467pp. That's about £40 more than La Plagne, which includes dinner and wine every night.

*Les Deux Alpes is B&B, so we'd be buying dinner every night. £40 a head is an estimate. At £30 it's ~£1,397pp, at £50 ~£1,537pp. Without dinners it's £1,187.28pp (£9,498 for the group).

Why La Plagne is worth the extra

Dinner sorted every nightHalf board with free wine. No hunting for a table for 8, no splitting bills. Once you count dinners, it's actually the cheaper trip.
Ski out the front doorClip in at the hotel and ski back to it on a blue. Easy for everyone, whatever their level.
Twice the terrain~135 runs and 425 km with Les Arcs: 74 blues for improvers, 35 reds and 18 blacks for the confident lot.
Better for a mixed groupLinked blues mean beginners and stronger skiers can ride together. In Deux Alpes the easy runs are at the top, and beginners usually have to take the lift down.
Sleeping at 2,100 mMid-January at that height is about as snow-sure as the Alps get.
Shorter transferAbout an hour less on the coach each way.

Good to know

Equipment rental isn't included in either price. Sort out your own skis, boards and boots: rent in resort, book online in advance, or bring your own.
Lift passes are on top of the holiday price in both cases (6-day pass, shown above), but they are already included in the totals shown.
Tourist tax is paid at the hotel in euros: €21.21 per person at La Plagne (about £18.61), €7 per person at Les Deux Alpes (about £6.14). It is already included in the totals shown.
